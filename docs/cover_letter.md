# Cover Letter

**To the Editor-in-Chief, Scientific Reports** *(adjust salutation to the chosen journal)*

Dear Editor,

Please find enclosed our manuscript entitled *"SGK1 as a computational target for perioperative neurocognitive disorders: in silico traditional Chinese medicine screening and 100 ns molecular dynamics validation"* for consideration as an original research article.

Perioperative neurocognitive disorders lack molecular targets that are both mechanistically credible and tractable for small-molecule intervention. In prior cross-species work we nominated five hub genes; among them, SGK1 is the only one with an experimentally resolved kinase domain (PDB 7PUE), which makes it the sole candidate that can be carried from a bioinformatics hypothesis into a structure-based test without modelled structures. We report an in silico screen of traditional Chinese medicine monomers that advanced six compounds (luteolin, resveratrol, kaempferol, daidzein, naringenin, tanshinone IIA) alongside the reference SGK1 inhibitor GSK650394, and we validate all seven complexes by 100 ns molecular dynamics. Every complex remained stable (gyration-radius drift ≤ 0.035%) and every ligand stayed inside the ATP-binding pocket (minimal ligand–protein heavy-atom distance 0.14–0.31 nm). MM-PBSA binding free energies placed GSK650394 first (−38.2 ± 14.6 kcal/mol) with the six TCM monomers clustering at −21.1 to −33.3 kcal/mol, driven by van der Waals and hydrophobic terms.

This work is a purely computational validation. We have stated its limits plainly: no experimental assay was performed, the receptor was rebuilt from 7PUE, and the binding free energies are single-trajectory MM-PBSA estimates without entropy correction and are therefore qualitative rather than a calibration of K~d~ or IC~50~. We believe the manuscript fits the journal's remit of technically sound, methodologically rigorous research, and we have provided the input files, analysis scripts, and output tables in a version-controlled repository.

A large-language model was used for writing assistance and language polishing; the computational design, simulations, and all reported numerical results were produced by the author. No AI tool performed the calculations.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, not under consideration elsewhere, and that all authors have approved the submission.

Thank you for your consideration.
